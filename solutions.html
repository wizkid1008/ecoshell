How it works

We think plastic reduction should be the boring choice

Eggshell is the highest volume food waste stream nobody has industrialised. We mill it, treat it and compound it into a bio calcium masterbatch that behaves like the resin already in your line.

Take out the plastic, keep the part

Ecoshell replaces up to half the polymer in a finished good with bio calcium recovered from eggshell. Density, flow and cycle time stay inside the window your tooling was built for, so qualification is a materials change rather than a redesign.

It runs in PP, HDPE and PET, in injection moulding, extrusion, blow moulding and thermoforming.

Explore resins and compounds

Durable enough to come back

Bio calcium raises stiffness and heat deflection, which is what reusable formats actually need. The same compound supports refillable cosmetic packaging, returnable transit packaging and hard goods built for a second and third life.

Ecoshell is a member of the PREVENT Waste Alliance, working on reuse systems in markets where collection infrastructure is still being built.

See reusable formats

Back into the stream it came from

Ecoshell material stays recyclable in the existing PP, PE and PET streams. It is not a bioplastic and it does not need a separate industrial composting route, so it will not contaminate the bales your recycler already sorts.

That distinction is the one customers ask about most, and it is the reason the material clears eco certification where bioplastics often stall.
