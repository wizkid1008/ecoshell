Our story

A supply chain that already spans four markets

Ecoshell started with a simple observation: eggshells are one of the largest unused waste streams in the world. We found a way to turn that waste into a product that is economical and sustainable, meets recycling goals, and works as an easy drop-in solution for manufacturers.

Ecoshell began as a sourcing firm in Taiwan and became a sustainability solutions provider, bringing quality Taiwanese manufacturing to Western markets. Manufacturing sits in Taiwan. Commercial operations run from the United States, Canada, where the business operates as an Indigenous owned enterprise, and the United Kingdom.

We are looking for converters, distributors and capital partners who want a position in plastic reduction that does not depend on a new waste stream existing first.
